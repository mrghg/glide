- done:
  - `src/lpdm/config.py`: `output_grid.density_weighting: dry_air | none`, default `dry_air` (decision 0022).
  - `src/lpdm/air_density.py` (new): `ParticleDryAirDensity`. rho_d = (p - e)/(R_d T), e = q p/(0.622 + 0.378 q), R_d = 287.05. T, q and p_s are sampled trilinearly at the particle (same vertical lookup as the wind) from the step's met window, at the step's time weight (`_advection_alpha_wind_mean`, the midpoint of `_step_window_time`; a period channel is held at its window-end value as elsewhere). **Pressure at the particle:** the window has no 3-D pressure, so p(z) = p_s exp(-g z/(R_d Tv(z/2))), Tv = T(1 + 0.6077 q): the hypsometric relation of `vertical_grid.model_level_pressure_pa` (decision 0010), from the ground to the particle, with the layer-mean Tv by the midpoint rule (error < 1e-7 in p over 40 m; ~1e-5 at 1 km). Chosen over adding a pressure channel to the met cache (more host memory, a reader change) and over the bbox-mean level pressure the turbulence code uses (`metadata.pressure_level_hpa`, wrong by up to ~15% over mountains). The [6, Z, Y, X] (T, q, p_s at window start and end) stack is copied to the device once per window, each channel slice directly; per step it is tensor operations only, the time weight in a 0-d device buffer. On CUDA with GLIDE_COMPILE the core is `torch.compile`d (one compile; ~60 kernels to a few).
  - `src/lpdm/footprint_gridder.py`: `accumulate(..., inv_density=None)`; None is the old arithmetic, a tensor divides each contribution (via `where`, so inactive particles cannot leak NaN).
  - `src/lpdm/main.py`: both step paths pass the density; `t`, `q` (and `sp`) added to the reader's channels with `dry_air`; preflight refuses `dry_air` with a reader lacking them; store attrs and `run_metadata.json` `footprint_units` declare `density_weighting`, `units` (`s m3 kg-1` or `s`), `density_formula`, `to_surface_sensitivity`.
  - `src/lpdm/output_writer.py`: the store attrs are also set on the `footprint` variable, so `ds["footprint"]` carries them.
