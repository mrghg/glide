  - **Near-surface well-mixed error of the Hanna scheme at the shipped outer step (needs a human look before the freeze).** In the well-mixed setup of the figure (production `HannaScheme`, backward, horizontally uniform, uniform density, manual reflection at the BL top, 10,000 to 20,000 particles), the occupancy of the lowest 5% of the boundary layer relative to uniform, averaged over 2–6 h, is: neutral (h 1500 m, u* 0.4) 1.67 at Δt = 60 s, 1.30 at 30 s, 1.03 at 15 s, 1.01 at 7.5 s; stable (h 400 m, H −20 W m⁻²) 1.78, 1.43, 1.15, 1.07; convective (H +200) within 0.03–0.11 below 1. Static and dynamic sub-step paths agree (neutral 60 s: 1.64 vs 1.67). Refining the sub-step target does not converge: neutral at 60 s gives 1.16 with no sub-stepping (substep_c ≥ 2), 1.67 at the default 0.5 and 1.60 at 0.125 (raising max_substeps to 1000 changes nothing); at 15 s, 1.03 at the default and 1.19 at 0.125. Particle-noise s.e. ≤ 0.03. So more sub-steps make the near-surface excess worse, and only a shorter outer step removes it. The leading candidate is the documented approximation that σ, T_L, ∂σ²/∂z and the density gradient are held at their outer-step values across sub-steps (docs/physics.md §8, STATUS "Per-sub-step σ re-evaluation"), but the cause is not established. Why CI does not see it: `test_v1_well_mixed_hanna_backward_path` runs at Δt = 15 s and asserts only the interior (600–2400 m). If it holds in real runs, 0–40 m footprints at Δt = 60 s would be inflated. Suggest a task before the freeze (A5 or A8): rerun this diagnostic with per-sub-step σ/T_L re-evaluation, and add a near-surface well-mixed gate at the shipped Δt.
