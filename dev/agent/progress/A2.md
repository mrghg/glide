- proposed follow-ups:
  - A deposition position that includes the step's turbulent displacement, e.g. the mean of the step's start and end positions (`step_mean`), or a trapezoid split (half the weight at each end). In the A2 prototype (`outputs/agents/A2/proto.py`, same plume, paired particles, two seeds) the start/end mean gave near-field errors of 0.0014-0.006 at Δt and 0.0024 at 4Δt, against 0.018-0.020 / 0.068 for the RK2 midpoint and 0.021-0.022 / 0.091 for endpoint. Cheap on both paths (both positions are in hand); needs a spec decision because it is not the RK2 midpoint the spec names.

On the smoke case it adds +5% to the 0-40 m footprint of a 10 m inlet, almost all in the receptor cell. Would have affected A3/A5 if read as a strictly better quadrature.
