  - Plan: the A2 spec describes `midpoint` (the RK2 advection predictor) as "consistent with the second-order scheme", but the predictor excludes the step's turbulent and convective displacement, so in the vertical it is a start-of-step (left-rectangle) rule, not a midpoint. On the smoke case it adds +5% to the 0-40 m footprint of a 10 m inlet, almost all in the receptor cell. Caught by this task's smoke comparison, before merge; replaced by `step_mean` (decision 0026).
  - Plan (the revision itself): `step_mean` was chosen on the analytic plume (T_L = 100 s, Δt <= 20 s), where a step's vertical displacement is small. In strong near-surface turbulence at Δt = 60 s the mean of a step's two heights is biased away from the ground (-18% in the 0-40 m occupancy of an idealized well-mixed BL with u* = 1 m/s; -4 to -8% of the smoke 0-40 m footprint outside the receptor cell). Would have affected A5's sweep and A3 if read as a strictly better quadrature. Caught by this revision's smoke ring analysis and an idealized well-mixed check, before merge.
  - Own near-miss: the first multi-seed run of the plume metric included the farthest along-wind cell, which the particles reach only at the last step, inflating every error by ~0.024; caught because the numbers disagreed with the prototype, fixed before any number was recorded.

  - Test design: the analytic plume (u′ = 0, cell edges on step times) ranked step_mean above endpoint partly through that alignment; with the edges moved the ranking reverses at Δt = 5 s.
